# Bank of reference papers that I've used so far

Calculation of Printed Circuit Board Power Loop Stray Inductance in GaN or High di/dt Applications
https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/8337781
> Paper about how the physical geometry and width of the PCB traces directly dictate parasitic stray inductance. Confirms that the only way to prevent overvoltage ringing and electromagnetic interference is by placing ground lines around the PCB and minimizing the magnetic loop area.
